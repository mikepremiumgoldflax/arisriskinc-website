MethodologyJune 2026

Seal the forecast. Publish the grade.

A history fit can look like a forecast. ARIS treats them as different objects. The forecast is sealed before the season, the method that will grade it is sealed, and the grade is published whatever it says.

A fit to past seasons is a description of those seasons. The choices that produced it — which features, which thresholds, which version shipped — were available to people who had already watched the outcomes. That record is useful. It is not, by itself, a commitment to the next season.

The test ARIS runs is a sealed one: the forecast exists before the season, and the grade is published from that record.

What gets sealed

Three things, in order.

  • The forecast, parcel by parcel, before the season it describes. The 2026 California forecast was sealed on that basis.
  • The grading method, sealed with the forecast, so the score is not redesigned after the fires.
  • The grade, published whatever it says.

The results are 100% reproducible bit-for-bit. The public description of the architecture is physics-based, two-stage, sealed and graded. This page is the discipline. The grade itself is published from the sealed record when the season has been scored.
