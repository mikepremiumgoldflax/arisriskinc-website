Privacy

Last updated 29 October 2026. This page covers the public website. It is not a description of a customer data room.

Who we are

ARIS Risk Inc. is based in Saskatchewan. Questions about this page go to partnerships@arisriskinc.com.

Email

The site has no submission form. If you email us, we receive whatever you send — name, company, role, and the note — and we use it to reply. We do not sell that correspondence.

Visit measurement

Pages on this marketing site load Google Analytics. The homepage also loads a Dealfront (Leadfeeder) script, which can associate a visit with a company network. Those providers process that visit data under their own terms. It is separate from the catastrophe model.

Model data

Model data is stored in Canada. The public description of the hardware is dedicated compute in Canada. That storage is not the same system as website analytics.

What this site does not do

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not run a shopping cart. We do not sell personal information.
