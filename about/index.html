About

A catastrophe model company.

ARIS builds wildfire catastrophe models that can be checked rather than believed.

The model is physics-based and parcel-resolved. It simulates fire across fuel, terrain and weather over ten thousand simulated seasons, estimates the probability each structure is reached and lost, and produces exceedance-probability output in open insurer standards.

The forecast is sealed before the season. The method for grading it is sealed. The grade is published whatever it says. Results are 100% reproducible bit-for-bit. In public, the architecture is physics-based, two-stage, sealed and graded.

The same engine

Pricing, mitigation and response come from one model. Pricing-grade parcel scores feed portfolio and accumulation views. Before the season the engine targets mitigation and supports the plan. After the work is done, it rescores the parcel. Response intelligence is a concept demonstration, available in 2027.

Where the work sits

The model was built in California. It is being extended to the Canadian Prairies for 2027, delivered with Saskatchewan field partners. ARIS is based in Saskatchewan. Model data is stored in Canada, on dedicated compute in Canada.

In California, twelve mitigation measures can bear on rates. That is directional. It is not a requirement to buy a model.
