The model

Wildfire catastrophe models you can check.

ARIS builds wildfire catastrophe models that can be checked rather than believed. Physics-based and parcel-resolved: the model simulates fire across fuel, terrain and weather over ten thousand seasons, estimates the probability each structure is reached and lost, and writes exceedance-probability output in the open standards insurers and reinsurers already use.

What the engine does

One engine. Price the risk, then use the same run.

Mitigation is not a second product. Targeting, planning and rescoring run on the model that prices the portfolio. Response intelligence is a concept demonstration, available in 2027.

Price the risk

Pricing-grade scores for each parcel. Portfolio and accumulation views. Exceedance-probability output in the open standards insurers and reinsurers already work with.

Mitigation, on the same engine

Before the season, target the work that changes the probability a structure is reached and lost. Plan that work. After it is done, rescore the parcel. In California, twelve mitigation measures can bear on rates. That guidance is directional. It is not a requirement to buy a model.

During a fire

Response intelligence is a concept demonstration. It is available in 2027. It is the same engine, used while an event is underway, not a separate product line.
